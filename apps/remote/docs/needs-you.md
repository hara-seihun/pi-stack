# Personal decisions and questions

[Attention](attention.md) displays the read-only, own-person projection at `GET /v1/needs-you`. Undated/due items appear in Now; future deadlines join calendar events in Upcoming. `#/needs-you` resolves to `#/attention`. It combines current open life decisions/person-only actions, person-owned waiting commitments and pending asynchronous questions from all of the person's authorized thread owners, including archived threads. It does not list Kenan-owned commitments, ordinary attention notices or watch conditions as human tasks.

Each thread owner serves `pendingQuestions({locationThreadIds})` directly from its partial pending-question index. One fresh query returns pending questions and their owners, plus only the current life records' requested location owners. Archived questions remain pending; answered/dismissed rows leave the result immediately. Remote excludes room owners and retains successful owners alongside explicit per-owner/per-thread failures. The read does not traverse question-event history, enumerate all threads or hydrate their execution/transcript state. `scripts/pending-questions-benchmark.mjs OWNER_DATABASE` compares the old scan's row/request counts with the current SQL using a read-only connection and metadata-only output.

The owning supervisor authenticates the caller through its existing router/local caller boundary. It uses `kenan-memory/life-client` with its own supervisor credential and a fixed `target: {scope: "self"}`; caller-supplied person/scope parameters are rejected. Rooms cannot read this projection. The endpoint creates no model calls or mutations.

Every item shows its consequence, required-by timestamp with timezone, and Kenan recommendation. Missing values are explicitly unknown. A life decision linked to a pending question enriches that question instead of making another inbox entry. Answers remain in the original question owner's durable store. Links identify both the conversation and question, and the existing question composer puts the selected question first. An answered question is not revived by a lagging open life decision.

**Dismiss** uses authenticated `POST /v1/needs-you/dismiss`, not local hiding. Its closed target is a life item/commitment ID with the displayed revision, or an original thread/question ID. Life needs transition to `dismissed` through own-person CAS. Questions are dismissed by their original owner, including questions linked to life needs; rooms remain excluded. A stale card cannot overwrite a changed life record. Partial question/life effects remain explicit and refresh the sources.

Dismissing a waiting commitment hides its reminder, not the obligation. A dismissed life need records the exact commitment revision it hides; standalone reminders create a dismissed needs-you record in the same encrypted life aggregate. The commitment stays waiting, and a changed revision resurfaces. Dismissal does not mutate policy, coverage or another person's life. The button stays disabled during submission, failed requests preserve the card, and confirmed mutations reload the sources.

Coverage is separate from items, under Attention's expandable **Coverage and delegation**. Life sources retain their actual check/reconciliation receipts and freshness deadlines; a GET only records when the view was read. Empty coverage, partial questions, stale receipts and unavailable owners stay visible. Watch coverage shows condition count and next scheduled due, with last actual check explicitly unknown because watch scheduling does not record a life reconciliation receipt. A next wake is not evidence of completed work.

The policy section reads the current own-person version without granting or editing authority. Corrections and revocation go through the person's Kenan and existing life policy owner. No second policy/answer/task store lives in Remote.

Owners: `server/needs-you.ts` derives the view, `server/needs-you-dismissal.ts` owns validated dismissal and reminder-version semantics, `shared/needs-you.ts` owns its wire contract, `web/src/needs-you.tsx` renders its cards and coverage/policy details within `web/src/attention.tsx`. The life aggregate and its source receipts remain owned by `packages/kenan-memory`; questions and watches remain with the existing thread/watch owners.
